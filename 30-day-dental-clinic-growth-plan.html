30-Day Dental Clinic Growth Plan

Turn local attention into booked patient appointments.

A practical 30-day plan to get more new patient leads, especially for implants, dentures, emergency dental, cleanings, and cosmetic dentistry.

Best services to scale first

Dental implants Dentures Emergency dental Cosmetic dentistry New patient exams

Week 1: Set Up the Foundation

Build the offer, landing page, lead storage, follow-up, and tracking.

Day 1: Pick the main offer

Choose one strong offer such as Free Implant Consultation, $99 New Patient Exam + X-rays, Emergency Dental Same-Day Appointments, or Denture Help Consultation.

Day 2: Build the landing page

Create a simple page with clinic name, main offer, services checklist, patient form, phone number, Book Appointment button, reviews, before/after proof, and doctor photo.

Day 3: Set up lead storage

Save every lead into Supabase: full name, phone, email, service selected, preferred appointment date, message, and lead source.

Day 4: Create ads

Make three simple campaigns for implant patients, denture patients, and emergency dental patients.

Day 5: Create follow-up scripts

Prepare text message, phone call, email, and missed call follow-up scripts.

Day 6: Set up tracking

Track leads, calls, booked appointments, show-ups, and closed treatment plans.

Day 7: Test everything

Submit a test lead and confirm the form works, the lead saves in Supabase, the clinic gets notified, and the patient gets confirmation.

Week 2: Launch the Campaign

Start local ads, post useful content, contact every lead fast, and review early results.

Day 8: Start Facebook and Instagram ads

Target people near the clinic interested in dental implants, loose dentures, missing teeth, emergency pain, and smile makeover services.

Day 9: Launch Google Search ads

Use high-intent keywords like dentist near me, dental implants near me, emergency dentist, dentures near me, and tooth pain help.

Day 10: Post on social media

Post a doctor intro, patient problem, service explanation, offer, and reviews.

Day 11: Add transportation message

Add: “Need help getting to your appointment? Ask us about ride support options.”

Day 12: Call every lead fast

Every lead should be contacted within 5 minutes.

Day 13: Retarget website visitors

Show ads again to people who visited but did not book.

Day 14: Review results

Check which ads got leads, which services got interest, and which leads booked.

Week 3: Improve and Scale

Cut weak campaigns, strengthen winners, and improve the landing page.

Day 15: Turn off weak ads

Stop ads that are not getting leads.

Day 16: Improve best ads

Make more versions of the winning ad.

Day 17: Add denture-focused campaign

Target people tired of loose dentures, painful dentures, trouble eating, embarrassment smiling, and denture glue.

Day 18: Add implant education post

Explain: “Implants can help replace missing teeth and make eating easier.”

Day 19: Add reviews/testimonials

Use real patient reviews if allowed.

Day 20: Improve landing page

Make the page easier with bigger buttons, a shorter form, a clear service checklist, and a strong call-to-action.

Day 21: Review bookings

Track how many leads became appointments.

Week 4: Close More Patients

Improve follow-up, reminders, financing language, and the next month’s growth plan.

Day 22: Add missed-lead follow-up

Follow up with leads who did not answer: Day 1 text, Day 2 call, Day 3 email, and Day 5 final text.

Day 23: Add financing message

Use simple wording: “Monthly payment options may be available.”

Day 24: Create patient FAQ

Answer cost, implant comfort, denture help, emergency patient availability, and online booking questions.

Day 25: Train front desk

Make sure staff knows how to answer lead calls, explain the offer, book quickly, and reduce no-shows.

Day 26: Send reminders

Send appointment reminders 24 hours before, the morning of appointment, and 2 hours before.

Day 27: Push strongest offer

Put the best offer on the website, ads, social posts, and Google Business Profile.

Day 28: Review numbers

Look at total leads, cost per lead, appointments booked, patients showed, and revenue opportunities.

Day 29: Make next month plan

Keep what worked. Remove what did not.

Day 30: Scale

Increase budget only on what is working, starting with implants, dentures, emergency dental, cosmetic dentistry, and new patient exams.
