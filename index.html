Patient questions

Common implant consultation questions.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Many patients are candidates, but bone support, gum health, medical history, and treatment goals should be reviewed before making a recommendation.

How long does treatment take?

Timing varies by case. Some patients need extra preparation or healing time, while others can move through the process more quickly.

Will the implant look natural?

The final restoration is planned to blend with your smile, bite, facial features, and neighboring teeth.

Can this replace dentures?

Implant-supported options can improve stability and comfort for many denture patients. The right solution depends on your anatomy and goals.
